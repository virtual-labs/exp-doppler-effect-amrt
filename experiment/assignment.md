## Assignment


<ol>
  <li>
    An ambulance travels down a highway at a speed of 75.0 mi/h, its siren emitting sound at a frequency of 400 Hz. What frequency is heard by a passenger in a car travelling at 55.0 mi/h in the opposite direction as the car:
    <ol type="a">
      <li>Approaches?</li>
      <li>Moves away from the ambulance?</li>
    </ol>
  </li>
  <li>
    A train moving at a speed of 40.0 m/s sounds its whistle, which has a frequency of 500 Hz. Determine:
    <ul>
      <li>The frequency heard by a stationary observer as the train approaches the observer.</li>
      <li>The frequency heard by the stationary observer as the train recedes from the observer.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>

  <li>
    A police car with its siren on (emitting at 600 Hz) is moving towards a stationary observer at a speed of 25.0 m/s. The speed of sound in air is 343 m/s.
    <ul>
      <li>What frequency does the observer hear as the car approaches?</li>
      <li>What frequency is heard as the car passes and moves away from the observer?</li>
    </ul>
  </li>

  <li>
    A person is riding a bicycle at 10.0 m/s toward a stationary source of sound that emits a frequency of 300 Hz. The speed of sound is 340 m/s.
    <ul>
      <li>What frequency does the person hear while moving toward the source?</li>
      <li>What frequency would they hear if they were moving away from the source at the same speed?</li>
    </ul>
  </li>

  <li>
    An observer is in a car moving at 20.0 m/s behind another car that is also moving in the same direction at 15.0 m/s. The car ahead is emitting a horn sound at 500 Hz. The speed of sound is 343 m/s.
    <ul>
      <li>What is the apparent frequency heard by the observer in the trailing car?</li>
      <li>How would the frequency change if the observer’s car slowed to 10.0 m/s?</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ol>
